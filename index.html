Mountain athlete tools

Plan, test, record and learn from your own data

MTNATH is one web app for mountain athletes. Plan races and routes, run lab tests, record indoor sessions, and log and plan your training, all in one account that works on any computer or phone.

Principles

  1. Your data is yours. You own all of it, decide who sees what, and can take it out at any time.
  2. Physiology is complex, so look at it from many sides. Lactate, heart rate, muscle oxygen, sleep, HRV, food, blood tests and injuries make sense next to each other.
  3. The mountains change, and so do we. Conditions, weather and daylight change outside; fatigue, injuries and form change inside. Knowing how keeps us safe.
  4. Every athlete is unique. Training should follow your own capacities and responses, not averages or medians.
  5. Every number shows where it comes from. Estimates are marked, and the methods are published. The tools inform you; you and your coach decide.
  6. Learn from your own history. Injuries, close calls, blood tests and past seasons are a record to look back on. Patterns show over years, not weeks.

The tools

🏁

Race planner

Pacing with grade-adjusted pace, aid stations, fuelling and a gut-load plan for every climb and descent.

🗺️

Route planner

Draw or import routes, read the elevation profile with timing, weather and marks.

🧪

Lab tests

Metabolic and GAP tests, zones, thresholds, blood tests, injuries and close calls.

⏺️

Recorder

Record lab and indoor sessions from Bluetooth sensors, add lactate and RPE as you go.

📓

Training log

Training, sleep, food and health in one log, with time in zones and your performance curve.

📅

Training plan

Season view, a session calendar and a library, shared live with your coach.

📈

Visualizer and Compare

Merge .fit and .csv files and lay activities on top of each other.

🔗

Connections

Wearables feed the log; connect your own Claude to ask questions across your data.
