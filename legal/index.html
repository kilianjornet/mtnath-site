1. Privacy policy

In short: your training and health data belong to you. We store it only to run the app for you, never sell it, show it only to people you choose, and you can download or delete it at any time.

Who is responsible

The data controller is JORNET BURGADA MOUNTAIN SPORTS (org. nr. 933 516 067), Hovdevegen 149, 6386 Måndalen, Norway. Contact for privacy questions: lab@mtnath.com.

What we collect

DataWhy
Account: email address, name, role, chosen toolsTo sign you in with an email link and run your account.
Training and planning: races, routes, training plan and log, activity files (.fit, .csv), comparisonsTo provide the tools you use.
Physiology and health: lab tests, zones and thresholds, sleep, resting HR, HRV, weight, food and hydration, blood tests, supplements and medication, injuries, close callsTo show, calculate and plan from your own data. Only what you enter or connect.
Connected services: data sent by COROS (activities, sleep, resting HR, nightly HRV) and Withings (weight), and the date you gave consentTo fill your training log automatically, only after you connect them.
Technical: sign-in sessions and server logs (IP address, browser, time)To keep the service secure and working.

Data about your health (such as blood tests, injuries, medication and heart rate) is a special category of personal data. We process it only because you choose to enter or connect it, on the basis of your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time by deleting the data or your account. Everything else is processed to provide the service you signed up for (contract) and, for security logs, our legitimate interest in keeping the app safe.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, on this site or in the app.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.

Services you connect yourself

  • COROS and Withings: data comes in only after you connect them in Account → Connections. You can disconnect at any time, and choose to keep or delete what was synced. Their own privacy policies apply on their side.
  • Claude connector: if you add MTNATH to your own Claude account, Claude can read (not change) the data of the tools you have turned on, under your own agreement with Anthropic. Disconnect removes its access at once.
  • Coach link and race share links: anyone with the link sees what you ticked. You can make a new link or stop sharing at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete data, or your account, it is removed from the app straight away and from backups within 30 days. Server logs are kept for 30 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct, download and delete your data, most of it directly in the app. You can also ask us to restrict or stop processing, or to send your data to another service. Write to lab@mtnath.com and we will answer within one month. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet, or the authority where you live.

Age

The app is for people aged 16 and over.

This website

mtnath.com is a static website with no accounts, forms or cookies. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and, on the Methods page, the MathJax library from jsDelivr, which means your browser connects to those services and they see your IP address.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it applies.

2. Terms of use

The service

MTNATH gives you tools to plan, record and analyse mountain training and racing. MTNATH is free to use. We may change, add or remove features, and will tell you in advance if a change removes something you rely on.

Not medical advice, and the mountains are your call

The app's numbers are models and estimates, not measurements or medical advice. Pacing, fuelling, gut-load, zones, blood-test ranges and mountain-condition information can be wrong. Check them, use your own judgement, and ask a doctor about health questions. Decisions in training, racing and in the mountains are yours, and so are the risks.

Your account and content

  • Keep access to your email safe: a sign-in link opens your account.
  • Everything you put in the app stays yours. You give us only the permission needed to store it and run the tools for you.
  • Do not misuse the service: no attempts to access other people's data, overload the app or break its security.
  • You can delete your account at any time. We may close an account that breaks these terms, after telling you why when we can.
